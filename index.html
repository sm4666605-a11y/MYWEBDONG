Formulas worth remembering

Stress

σ = P / A

Force divided by cross-sectional area.

Strain and Young's modulus

ε = ΔL / LE = σ / ε

Stiffness of a material in its elastic range.

Beam reactions, central point load

R = P / 2Mmax = PL / 4

For a simply supported span.

Beam under uniform load

R = wL / 2Mmax = wL² / 8

Maximum moment sits at mid-span.

Bending stress

σ = M y / I

Largest at the outer fibre, zero at the neutral axis.

Equilibrium

ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0
